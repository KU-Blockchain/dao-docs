# Welcome to the KUBI Docs!

These docs are a living, collectively created set of resources to make KUBI's internal tools, especially the DAO, accessible to all audiences.

This page gives an overview of the DAO for all audiences. To get a deeper understanding of how you can participate in the DAO, check out our resource guides for both technical and non-technical audiences.

## What is KUBI? 

The KU Blockchain Institute is a student-led organization of innovators at the University of Kansas working on open source research projects, industry-focused events, and technical workshops.

- **The DAO**: our decentralized governance platform, where members earn rewards for contributing to tasks and vote on proposals that shape the institution.
- **Projects**: open-source and applied engineering work that members build, ship, and learn from.
- **Trips**: travel opportunities (conferences, hackathons, and more) that top members become eligible for.
- **Events**: workshops, meetings, and socials that make up the day-to-day of being a KUBI member.

Everything in these docs is organized around helping you participate in one or more of these pieces.

While you don't need to deeply understand all of this to get started, we've also created resources on things like the [history of the KUBI DAO](https://docs.kublockchain.com/blog/history-of-the-KUBI-DAO) so you can understand not just how to use the DAO, but the intentions behind its creation and its benefits to our community.

## Why do we use the DAO?

1. Enabling Democracy: The DAO is a tool for ensuring every KUBI member has an equal opportunity to be recognized for their contributions.
2. Empowering Developers: Giving members structured opportunities for hands-on learning and experimentation in web3 through open source contribution.
3. Incentivizing Learning and Engagement: Rewarding the effort and impact that simple contributions like learning and attending meetings have on our community.

## Why should I participate in it?

The core of all of KUBI's decisions runs through the DAO — it's how the club recognizes and rewards the people actually doing the work. Contributing puts you on the leaderboard, and the leaderboard is what the community uses to recognize its most active members.

Moving up the leaderboard will also make you eligible for exclusive travel opportunities. You can read about our [trips](#trips) above to see what past contributors have gotten to do.

## How can I participate in the DAO?

All DAO activity — creating your account, completing tasks, and checking the leaderboard — happens on the [KUBI DAO site](https://dao.kublockchain.com/home/).

1. Go to [dao.kublockchain.com](https://dao.kublockchain.com/home/).
2. Click **Join or Sign In** in the top corner.
3. Fill in the exact sign-up steps (email/KU email sign-in, wallet connect, verification, etc.).
4. Once you're in, you'll land on your dashboard, where you can browse open tasks, submit contributions, and track your position on the leaderboard.

For a deeper walkthrough of the DAO itself — how rewards work, what counts as a contribution, and how to read the leaderboard — see [Get Started with the DAO](/docs/get-started/overview).
